隐私政策

饭钱账本 · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 18 日

饭钱账本是一款个人开发的免费 App。我们非常重视你的隐私：这个 App 不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何个人数据。

你的记录

你记下的餐厅、日期、金额、小费、备注和位置，只保存在你自己的 iPhone 上。我们没有服务器，看不到你的任何数据。删除 App 会同时删除这些数据。

相机和照片

当你选择“拍小票”或“从相册选”时，App 会在你的手机上识别小票上的文字，用来自动填写金额。照片不会被保存到 App 外，也不会上传到任何地方。

位置

记一笔时，App 会使用你的位置来找到你所在的餐厅，并在足迹地图上点亮对应的州。查找餐厅和地址时会使用 Apple 地图服务，相关请求由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 隐私政策。我们不会保存或接收你的位置。你可以随时在“设置 → 饭钱账本 → 位置”里关闭定位，App 的其他功能仍可正常使用。

没有账号、广告和追踪

App 不需要注册账号，不含广告，不使用任何第三方统计或追踪工具。

儿童

App 不会有意收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下的儿童。

政策变更

如果这份政策有变化，会更新在本页面并修改上面的日期。

Privacy Policy

Meal Ledger · Last updated: October 18, 2026

Meal Ledger is a free app made by an independent developer. The app does not collect, upload or share any of your personal data.

Your records

The restaurants, dates, amounts, tips, notes and locations you log are stored only on your iPhone. There is no server, and we cannot see any of your data. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Camera and photos

When you scan a receipt or choose a photo, the app reads the text on your device to fill in the amounts. Photos are not saved outside the app and are never uploaded.

Location

When you log a meal, the app uses your location to find the restaurant you are at and to light up the matching state on your map. Restaurant and address searches use Apple Maps; those requests are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We never store or receive your location. You can turn off location at any time in Settings → Meal Ledger → Location; the rest of the app keeps working.

No accounts, ads or tracking

The app needs no account, shows no ads, and uses no third-party analytics or tracking.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
